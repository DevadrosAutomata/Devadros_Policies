3.2 Location Information

With your explicit permission, the Application accesses your device's precise location to identify nearby hospitals and pharmacies, calculate routes between your current location and selected destinations, and provide location-based services and route guidance. Your location information is processed only for providing these services and is not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes. You may disable location access through your device settings at any time. However, doing so may prevent certain features of the App from functioning properly.

Route Information

When you request directions through the Application, your current location and selected destination are processed to generate an appropriate travel route. Route information is processed solely to provide routing and navigation functionality and is not retained as part of your account history unless otherwise expressly stated in this Privacy Policy or required by applicable law.
